## Transfer and synchronization finding

`SimpleCustomBatch.cuda()` uses blocking `.to(device)` for every tensor despite
the DataLoader already using `pin_memory=True`.

| Transfer | Count | Copy time | Host-side synchronization |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pinned H2D | 30 | 0.31ms | 180.92ms |
| Pageable H2D | 28 | 0.06ms | 0.38ms |
| D2H | 8 | 0.02ms | 0.06ms attributed to scalar extraction |

This makes `non_blocking=True` on the pinned batch transfer the first isolated
optimization experiment.
